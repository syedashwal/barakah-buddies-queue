Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Barakah Buddies Uploader ("the tool") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Barakah Buddies YouTube channels to upload and schedule the owner's own videos. This policy explains what data the tool uses.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

  • OAuth tokens for the channel owner's own Google account, used only to upload videos, set video details and add videos to playlists on the owner's channels.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and title) to choose the right channel.

We do not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube user. We do not use cookies or analytics on this site.

How we use and store data

Tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used only to perform uploads. Video files are kept in the repository only until they are uploaded, then removed. We do not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the repository secrets within 7 days.

Children

Our videos are made for children and are uploaded as "made for kids". The tool never collects information from children or any viewer.
