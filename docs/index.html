Barakah Buddies is a family of YouTube channels with short, gentle Islamic-manners cartoons for children aged 2 to 6, in English, Urdu, Hindi and Tamil.

Barakah Buddies Uploader is a small internal tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads our own finished Shorts to our own four channels and sets their scheduled publish time, title, description and playlist. It does not access any other user's data, and there are no outside users.

▶ YouTube This tool uses YouTube API Services.

Our channels

  • Barakah Buddies English
  • Barakah Buddies Urdu
  • Barakah Buddies Hindi
  • Barakah Buddies Tamil

How it works

  1. Finished videos and their titles are placed in this GitHub repository.
  2. A scheduled GitHub Action signs in with the channel owner's own Google account (OAuth 2.0, scope youtube.upload/youtube).
  3. It calls videos.insert with a future publishAt time and selfDeclaredMadeForKids=true, then playlistItems.insert to add the video to the right playlist.
